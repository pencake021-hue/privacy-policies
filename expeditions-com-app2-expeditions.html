About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains what happens with information when you use the mobile application Expeditions (package name com.app2.expeditions, the "App"), which is published on Google Play by Neltner Realty ("we", "us" or "our"). By using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Information we collect

We do not ask you for, and the App does not collect, any personal information. The App does not request your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or any other personal data.

The App needs internet access only for the purposes described in the sections "Log data" and "Website shown inside the App" below.

Any simple settings or progress that the App keeps (for example your chosen options or quiz results) stay on your own device. We do not receive them and they are not sent anywhere.

Log data

Each time the App is opened it makes one small request over the internet to a settings file on our website. This file only tells the App whether the website feature is switched on or off. No personal data, device identifiers or user content are included in that request.

As with any request on the internet, the server that answers it can see standard technical details that your device sends automatically, such as your IP address, the type of device or browser software, and the date and time of the request. This kind of log data is created by the web hosting service, we do not use it to identify you, and we do not combine it with any other information about you.

Website shown inside the App

The App can show pages from our website (https://meteorousastro.click) inside the App, in the same way a web browser would. While a page is open, that website receives the same standard technical details described under "Log data". The App itself does not add any tracking or analytics to those pages.

Like any browser component, the Android system may store small files such as cookies on your device for the pages you open. You can remove them at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Cookies

The App itself does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies. Cookies can only be created by web pages shown inside the App (if that feature is on), as described above.

Service providers

We do not hire any third-party company to collect or process personal data for the App. The only outside services involved are the web hosting service that delivers the settings file and website pages mentioned above, and Google Play, the store through which you download the App. Google handles information about your download and your Google account under its own privacy policy.

Security

We value your trust. Because the App does not collect personal data, there is none for us to store or lose. Nevertheless, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Links to other sites

The App or the pages shown in it may contain links to websites that we do not operate. If you open such a link you will leave the App and go to that third party's site. We have no control over these sites and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please read the privacy policy of every site you visit.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no personal information is collected through the App, there is nothing to remove, but if you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us using the details below.

Your rights

Since we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, or you have any request about your privacy, contact us and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will publish the new version on this page and change the effective date at the top. Please check this page occasionally. Changes take effect as soon as they are posted.
